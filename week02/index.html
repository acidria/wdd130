Kampala, Uganda

Kampala is the capital and largest city of Uganda in the central region along the northern shores of Lake Victoria. It is approximately 1,200 metres above sea level and is characterised by rolling hills and valleys. Kampala is Uganda's political, administrative, commercial, and cultural centre and serves as an important gateway to the wider East African region.
