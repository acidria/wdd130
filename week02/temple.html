Kampala,Uganda

Kampala is the capital and largest city of kampala in the central region of Uganda along the Northern shores of lake Victoria. It is approximately 1200 metres above sea level and is characterised by rolling hills and valleys. Kampala is Uganda's political,administrative, commercialand cultural centre and serves as an important gateway to the wider East African Region.
